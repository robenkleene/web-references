# Slack

- `⌘k`: Quick switch to room
- `⌥↑` or `⌥↓`: Next or previous channel
- `⇧⌥↑` or `⇧⌥↓`: Next or previous unread channel
- `⌘.`: Toggle right pane
- `⌥↓`: Down conversation
- `⎋`: Remove unread indication for this room
- `⇧⎋`: remove unread indication for all rooms

## Messages

- Only *messages* can be marked as unread, not `Channels` or `Direct messages` rooms. To mark a message as unread choose `Mark unread` from the contextual menu.

## Notifications

- `Preferences > Notifications > Sounds & Appearance: Show a badge on Slack's icon to indicate new activity`: Toggling this off turns off the badge icon *only for `Channels`*, i.e., the badge will still show for `Direct messages`, even if this is off
- `Preferences > Mark as read > When I view a channel > Start me where I left off, and mark the channel read`: So that clicking a channel always marks it as read

## Notes

In Slack, `@channel` sends notifications to everyone and @here sends notifications only to people that are currently active

## Previews

Previews, e.g., for blog posts, will only show up if the image assets are less than 2 MB.
